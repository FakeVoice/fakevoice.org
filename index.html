Why FakeVoice exists

A few seconds of someone's speech is now enough for AI to produce a convincing copy of their voice. Scammers use this to call people while sounding like a child, a parent, or a friend.

Warnings about this are easy to forget. Hearing it for yourself is not. FakeVoice is a short, safe experience that lets you hear the problem first-hand and leaves you with three simple habits.

FakeVoice is an independent educational project. It is not a voice-cloning service.

FakeVoice

How your voice is handled

  • FakeVoice only ever calls back the number that called it. You cannot enter anyone else's number.
  • Your voice is processed temporarily, for this one session only.
  • Nothing can be downloaded, exported, or shared, by you or anyone else.
  • You cannot type text for the voice to say. What it says is fixed in advance.
  • No reusable voice model is kept. Each session starts from nothing.
  • The recording and the generated voice are deleted right after the callback, and never kept longer than 10 minutes. The site only tells you they are deleted after the system confirms it.
  • On the last screen you can play two short clips, your recording and the AI voice, side by side. Only your session can play them, they can't be downloaded, and they are deleted when you leave that screen or press Delete, and never kept longer than 10 minutes.

Service providers

We use these providers only to run the experience:

  • Twilio (phone calls; keeps call records such as numbers, times and durations under its own policy)
  • Deepgram (real-time speech-to-text during the call)
  • OpenAI (generates the questions' follow-ups during the first call)
  • Fish Audio (creates the temporary voice copy and speaks it; we delete the copy through its API)
  • Cloudflare and GitHub (website hosting and network)
