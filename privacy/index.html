Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

FakeVoice ("we") runs fakevoice.org and the FakeVoice phone experience. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and who processes it for us.

Who can use FakeVoice

FakeVoice is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should know you are using it. FakeVoice is not available to people in Illinois.

We do not sell or share your information for advertising, we do not use it to train AI models, and we do not let anyone download your recording or voice copy. Your voice copy is never used to call anyone except you, once, in the callback you asked for.

We do not keep transcripts of your call. During the call, speech is converted to text in real time so the AI can respond; that text is not stored by us.

Service providers

We use these providers only to run the experience:

  • Twilio (phone calls; keeps call records such as numbers, times and durations under its own policy)
  • Deepgram (real-time speech-to-text during the call)
  • OpenAI (generates the questions' follow-ups during the first call)
  • Fish Audio (creates the temporary voice copy and speaks it; we delete the copy through its API)
  • Cloudflare and GitHub (website hosting and network)

Your choices

  • Don't check the consent boxes, and nothing is recorded.
  • Hang up at any time; anything recorded so far is deleted.
  • Questions or deletion requests: hello@fakevoice.org.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if anything changes.
